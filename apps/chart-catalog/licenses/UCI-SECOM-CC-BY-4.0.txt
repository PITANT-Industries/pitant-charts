PITANT extracted each original row number and recorded October line-test outcome
and added a model score from class-balanced logistic regression fitted on the
preceding July–September rows. The model uses train-only median imputation,
constant-feature removal and standardization. The record outcomes are retained
from the source, where +1 means failure and −1 means pass. Scores are rounded to
eight decimal places for this example and are uncalibrated. The catalog displays
the 359 October holdout rows, including all 22 recorded failures.
