Experiments, Virginia Tech (2010).
https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/bitstream/handle/10919/37729/Freeman_LauraJ_D_2010.pdf#page=73

The fixture selects the 200 V, 300 V, and 350 V cells at 170 °C. It retains
the four observed failure hours in each cell and assigns each of its four
right-censored units the fourth failure's reported hour, following the
study's Type II stopping rule. Unit IDs are illustrative labels.
