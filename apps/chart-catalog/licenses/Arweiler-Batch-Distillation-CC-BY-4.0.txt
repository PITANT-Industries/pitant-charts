The startup overlay fixture adapts the experimental sensor time series and
interpolation flags. It retains all 30 runs at operating points 001–003 for
the 1-butanol, 2-propanol and water mixture. Channels T703 and T705 are
retained in degrees Celsius at 30-second intervals, at the first archived
Operation timestamp, and at the end of the shortened display window.
Duplicate clock readings use the last row, preferring Operation at its
shared boundary with Startup. Absent and source-interpolated observations
are represented as missing. Each display ends at the earliest of the
100-minute limit, the end of observed operation, or five minutes after the
first Operation record. Times are in elapsed seconds on the archived
per-run clock, with the first Startup row as the alignment event. The
45-minute comparison deadline is illustrative and was added for this
example. The adaptation contains no simulation predictions.
