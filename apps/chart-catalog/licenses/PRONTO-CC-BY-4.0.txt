PITANT extracted Test 3 at the Cranfield multiphase flow facility on 7 September
2017, 14:47–15:57 on the site log clock. The fixture converts timestamps to seconds
relative to the first retained record. Within each 60-row SCADA minute, elapsed
seconds follow the original 1 Hz record order. It retains every fifth record
from three channels, rounds values to four decimal places, and pairs the logged
activation and clearance of five instrument module alarms without changing their
millisecond precision. Successive manual V11 position settings define intervals
from the operation log's whole-minute times. No sensor values or alarm events are
simulated. The source experiment deliberately reproduced an inlet air blockage.
