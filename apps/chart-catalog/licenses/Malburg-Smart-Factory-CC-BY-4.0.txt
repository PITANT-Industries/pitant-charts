PITANT Charts transformation:
Use the published Cleaned Event Log/MainProcess.xes from the physical
University of Trier smart-factory demonstrator. Select traces first recorded
from 14:24:00 inclusive to 15:46:00 exclusive on 25 June 2021, as recorded
on the source log clock. Of 30 candidates, 21 have all six ordered boundaries,
have successful oven and storage completions, and start and end at the given
boundary events. The nine excluded cases have no terminal storage completion.
Derive one stage entry per recorded boundary. Use storage unload scheduled
and start to identify the observed queue. Other stages group work and transfers
between recorded boundaries, ending with successful storage completion.
Express the 126 timestamps as seconds from 14:24:00 and retain the case IDs.
The published fixture reports the selected cohort and contains no sensor
payload or service metadata. See scripts/extract-cumulative-flow.py.
