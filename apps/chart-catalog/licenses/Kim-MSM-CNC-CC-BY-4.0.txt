The PITANT example selects 16 passes from the February 26, 2024 laboratory
experiment imi_vm20i/dataset10. The annotated start and end times, speed, axial
depth, direction, and other cutting settings come from label.csv. The cohort
fixes AA 6061 material, three-flute Tool B (diameter 50.8 mm), 26.67 mm radial
depth and 63.5 mm/s programmed feed (150 in/min in G20 program B_N2-a3.nc).
Each speed and axial depth combination has one up-milling and one down-milling
pass. Row numbers follow the data rows of label.csv, starting at one.

The fixture copies the original time and watt values from
20240226_055737.825160_mtc.csv, including the enclosing measurements at each
annotated cut boundary. The source identifies watt as true power at the main
supply. Exported rows have a median interval of about 0.145 s. The meter
adapter's update interval is about 1 s and consecutive rows can repeat a value.
The chart integrates within annotated windows and skips intervals longer than
0.25 s. Pass 52 has a 0.461592 s gap at the end of its cut and is omitted from
the setting median. The denominator is one annotated cutting pass. The
comparison covers main-supply electricity within those windows and retains
baseline draw during the cuts.

Regeneration: run apps/chart-catalog/scripts/extract-energy-operating-map.py
with label.csv followed by the MTConnect CSV. Its SHA-256 checks pin both files.
