NASA Milling Wear

Published by the NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence (PCoE).
The source measurements were provided by the UC Berkeley Emergent Space
Tensegrities (BEST) Lab. Kai Goebel (NASA Ames) and Alice Agogino
(UC Berkeley) authored the experimental documentation.

The example contains numeric summaries of measured readings. The original
archive includes mill.mat and Milling-Readme.pdf and is available from:

Each row uses Case 11 of the mill structure in mill.mat, in ascending run
order 1 through 23. The experiment machined cast iron at 0.75 mm depth of
cut and 0.25 mm/rev feed. Compute arithmetic means over the inclusive range
of MATLAB samples 1801–7200 for smcAC, vib_spindle, and AE_table. The
readme describes hardware RMS conditioning of the vibration and acoustic
emission signals. Means are retained on the recorded signal scale and
rounded to twelve decimal places.

The example retrospectively fits a fixed reference to runs 1–8 and scores
runs 9–23. The example computes the control limit and flags from this
reference. The study recorded flank wear separately.
