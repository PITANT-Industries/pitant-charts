PITANT adapts the published normalized pre-automation work-element data in
the workbook's Yamazumi sheet, rows 4–20 and columns D–F. For each operator it
sums the displayed 0.1-second values for processes 1–4, 5–8, 9–12, and 13,
the irregular work, material replacement, and tag-recording rows as one
support parcel, and the walking row. Groups with no assigned time are omitted.
The adapted station loads are 9.5 s, 10.2 s, and 11.2 s. The sum for Operator 3
uses the element values. Its workbook total cell is 11.4 s.

The available time (37,800 s), demand (3,600 units), and station capabilities
are chart scenario inputs. The scenario keeps support and walking at the
source station, treats each routine parcel as transferable, and holds its
duration constant during a move. Precedence constraints and post-transfer
performance fall outside the recorded measurements. This example models the
published workload at the baseline and illustrates planning alternatives.
